# Wortkasten

German flashcards: spaced repetition, der/die/das colours, verb forms, plural notation, a built-in offline dictionary and pronunciation.

**Open the app:** use the link on the right (“About” → website) or `https://anourian86.github.io/wortkasten/`. On the first screen, tap **Install the app** to put it on your home screen.

Your cards are stored only on your own device and — if you choose — in your own Dropbox (folder *Apps/Wortkasten*). Nothing is stored on GitHub.
